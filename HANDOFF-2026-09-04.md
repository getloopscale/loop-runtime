## The architecture, and why it changed today

It was three tmux lanes (`squad1/2/3`) plus a supervisor loop, a router, a dispatcher
and a lander. That arrangement was retired at 16:36 after being measured:

- **94% idle across 27 hours** — 1528 minutes idle against 92 productive.
- Route ran every ~50 minutes against a configured 10, because it shared a loop with a
  20-minute landing job.
- Every defect found that day was a **coordination** defect, not a work defect.

What replaces it: **one coordinating instance — you — that spawns subagents.** They are
created with a role, work in their own git worktree, and end when the work ends. There
is no lane to sit idle because there is no lane.

The observation surface is `/workflows` from inside here, plus two scripts for anyone
outside: `~/dev/squad/mechanisms/fleet/squad_status.sh --project ~/dev/loopzero` and
`workflow_watch.sh`.

## The fourteen roles

`~/dev/squad/agents/` carries fourteen role files. They are **mechanism, not
decoration**: each states what it decides and — more importantly — where its authority
ends. The line that admits a role is **measuring versus judging**: `fleet_lander` stays
a script because determinism is its protection; a component that reads and interprets
becomes an agent because reading is.

Three of the fourteen decide **nothing**, deliberately: `clio-historian` (what the
record says about the past), `metis-oracle` (what is true right now), and
`leonardo-researcher` (what a decision needs). Every role that decides has a reason to
want the evidence to read a particular way; those three do not.

Two you will use constantly:

- **`nemesis-claim-auditor`** — audits ASSERTIONS, not behaviour. Run her before you
  report anything. She has already caught, today: a claim that three items were freeable
  on the strength of one gate while the suite was red one gate further along; and an
  agent stating that **the sponsor personally made a commit that an agent made**. Both
  would have reached the operator as fact.
- **`aesculapius-healer`** — decides which recorded impediments have already been cured.
  A wall is written at a moment and nothing goes back to check.

## Rules that are not negotiable here

- **Never move a threshold, baseline or allowlist to make a gate pass.** Fix the cause,
  or refuse and say why. Refusing is a complete answer.
- **Never write `status:` in BACKLOG.md by hand** — use
  `~/dev/squad/mechanisms/cycle/backlog_status.py`, which enforces legal transitions.
- **Never delete a wall's reasoning** — replace it with what retired it.
- **Never free an item whose wall names a decision, an approval, elapsed time or host
  access.** Those do not expire; treating waiting as curing invents consent.
- **Never `git stash` in a worktree** (#31). Copy aside with `cp`, or commit on your
  branch, and restore with `git restore`.
- **Never claim a human did something an agent did.**
- Commits: English, no `Co-Authored-By` trailer, work on `workspace`.
