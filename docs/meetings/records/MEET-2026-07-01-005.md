**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Type:** multi-cell · database · billing (T2+) — focused tenant-guardian re-review of the ADR-5 enumeration revision

## Committee

## The defect + fix
v1.8 fixed the active-tenant SOURCE (phantom `tenants.status` → Tenant CR `status.phase`) but, by deleting the cell-bearing `ListActiveTenantsWithCell` query, forced cell resolution onto the mutable CR `spec.cellID` mirror — an INV I2 violation (PG `tenants.cell_id` is the canonical placement SoT; the CR mirror can diverge on partial pin / pre-reconcile → wrong cell → 0 pods → silent under-bill + double-cursor drift, Risk #6). The tenant-guardian WITHHELD on v1.8.

## Conditions folded into v1.9
- **C1 (BLOCKER, INV I2):** cell from canonical PG `tenants.cell_id` via NEW `GetCellForTenant :one` (SET LOCAL RLS-bypass; NoRows→skip); ADR-5 + T3.1 resolve cell from PG, NEVER the CR mirror; the CR list supplies ACTIVE-filter + tenant_id ONLY. Test `TestMeteringEnumeration_ResolvesCellFromPGNotCR` (PG-REAL) — closes Risk #6.
- **C2 (HIGH):** `TenantLister` scoped to canonical `loopzero-system` (ADR-0018) + filters the observed `status.phase` (not `spec`). Guard test asserts both.
- **C3 (LOW):** Active→Suspended drain-window under-count documented in § Drawbacks (intentional — suspended = don't bill).
