## Key Decisions

### GAP 1 Reclassified: Per-Environment Applications Sufficient for Launch

The ArgoCD blast radius incident was caused by Certificate CRs in the gitops tree. CF Zone Management v2 eliminates Certificate CRs from manifests. Per-project Applications deferred to post-100-tenants.

**Mitigation:** Manifest validation gate (P1) rejects known-problematic resource types.

### Leader Election: PG Keepalive Fix (not Lease migration)

Default PG `tcp_keepalives_idle` on DO managed PG is 7200s (2 hours). Fix: add keepalive params to connection string. Detects dead connections in ~90s instead of 2 hours.

### Preview Environments: P2 (DX dissent recorded)

DX Engineer dissented — argues preview envs drive Pro plan revenue and should be P1. Committee maintained: stability before features. Preview envs are FIRST P2 item.
