Ten pieces, four zones

Drawn last, from D1–D4. A component map produced first looks like design happened and forces no choice; this one is a summary of decisions already made, and the gate checks it against the declared pieces.

What the map says that the list did not. Cell provisioning sits beside the path, not on it — a cell is brought into existence once while an app deploys many times, and drawing them in sequence would put a one-time act on the hot path. The three state pieces are one zone and three components, because D4 gives them three different durability answers and two of those are still open. And PIECE-5 feeds PIECE-1 and nothing else: if the catalogue is also read at runtime, this map is missing an edge and is wrong.
