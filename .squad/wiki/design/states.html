The lifecycle of an Application

Six phases, read from operators/api/v1/application_types.go. Serving and Reachable are separate on purpose: a workload can be up before its route resolves.

Measured, not drawn from memory. The six phases and their write counts come from operators/internal: Cleaned 6, Progressing 5, Serving 3, Reachable 3, Down 2, Degraded 2. Open: no suspended phase exists for an idle agent, and no Draining phase exists for a platform upgrade — both are questions the sign-off carries rather than decisions this drawing makes.
