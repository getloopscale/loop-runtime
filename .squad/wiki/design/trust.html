Where customer code stops

The boundary is an account boundary, not a namespace one: the cell runs inside the customer's own AWS account. What crosses it is one credential per cell.

Measured. PIECE-9 states the cell is brought into existence inside the customer's AWS account. NetworkPolicy and securityContext are exercised by chart tests; ClusterCredential is one of the eight CRDs, which is how a cell authenticates rather than a shared secret. Open: no egress allowlist was found for the customer's app, and whether tenant isolation is namespace, node or cell was not established.
