From nothing to running, including what fails

PIECE-6 requires one route with no fork, and that every failure names what she must change. The four failure returns are drawn in coral — they are the requirement, not the exception handling.

Fourteen steps, four of them returns to the developer. That ratio is the point: PIECE-6 asks for one route where every failure names what to change, so a drawing of the happy path alone would have hidden the requirement rather than shown it. Open: whether the build runs in the customer's cell or on platform infrastructure — it decides whether a heavy build can starve a running agent.
